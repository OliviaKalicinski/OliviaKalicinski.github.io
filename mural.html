01 — growth

I own the acquisition P&L of a DTC brand.

Paid media, creators, CRM and CRO for a pet-food brand built on insect protein — and the landing pages, dashboards and automations underneath. I lead four people. I set budget by contribution margin per page, not ROAS, because ROAS never tells you when you are losing money per unit.

one

The average was hiding the account

Most of the month's purchases were coming from a single creator ad on a small slice of the spend — and it pointed traffic at one cheap item instead of the bundle. I read the distribution instead of the mean, found it, and changed the destination. Purchases and CPA both moved inside a week.

scale

Scaled without buying the usual trade-off

Scaling normally costs efficiency. I moved budget only into sets still clearing their page's margin ceiling, so spend went up and cost per purchase held — not the other way round.

ceiling

Changed how the company decides

Replaced ROAS with contribution margin per landing page: real order value minus COGS, shipping, fees and tax. Every page carries its own CPA ceiling.

artist statement

Clay fired in open pits,
with electronic waste inside.

I reflect on how we live today: busy, interconnected, and overwhelmed by endless stimuli. We consume objects, images, and information at an unrelenting pace, leaving behind traces that accumulate without much thought. Life moves quickly, and in the rush, we often lose sight of ourselves, each other, and the natural world around us.

Through clay shaped and fired in fire pits, combined with electronic waste, organic matter, and other discarded materials, I create sculptures, engravings, and installations that embody the tension between past, present, and future. My photographs also construct this narrative, reflecting on the cycle of life-death-life and capturing the subtle dialogues between human interventions and nature's responses.

Through my creative process, I offer a space for pause, estrangement, curiosity, and reflection. What are we doing to the world? And what is the world doing to us? I aim to inspire a critical perspective, encouraging a reconnection with the earth, with time, and with our essence.
