This is not a normal growth profile.

I have led marketing at Comida de Dragão since launch — a dog food made from insects, produced in the first insect biofactory in Rio de Janeiro. The category does not exist yet: no competitor to copy, no benchmark to beat, nobody out there searching for the product.

For a company that sells online, marketing is the engine. I lead it — acquisition, and the brand underneath: positioning, voice, and what the brand stands for. I listen to my team and to the people above me, and then I decide. One of those decisions changed how the company spends: budget now follows contribution margin per landing page, not ROAS.

I code, so a decision does not sit in a queue — the landing pages, the dashboards, the agents that read the numbers each morning. And I am an artist, selected for the Casero residency and awarded at Festival do Minuto in 2024: that is where the brand judgement comes from, learning to look at a work, say what it is actually doing, and find how concept and making hold together.
